# Formal local-XFS comparison: scale FAIL

Two order-balanced repeats of 1,000 simultaneous conversations per leg completed on `/dev/md0p1` XFS (`st_dev=66314`), using the unchanged [formal policy](../../../benchmarks/conversation_concurrency_contract.json). The pinned runner returned correctness, coverage and current-source qualification passes in all four legs. Independent verification confirms the readback, source inventory and arithmetic, but cannot establish historical SQLite SIGKILL delivery/exit or loaded-library identity stability across all boundaries. **Every leg exceeded the 1,000 ms scheduled-stream P99 budget.** All leg exits and the pair exit were 1; these were latency verdicts, not crashes, workload timeouts or missing recovery. No overall winner or DolphinFS conclusion follows.
